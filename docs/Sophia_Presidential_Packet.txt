Mr. President:

I write in good faith, as a citizen who has already begun this work with initial funding of one dollar ($1) and a full design for restoration—and to invite you to meet that work with an olive branch rather than indifference.

I have lost homes. I have known homelessness. I have come through addiction and withdrawal in a country that often showed no mercy. By the grace of God I climbed out, earned my way back to a roof, and then watched further harm through financial systems and policies that concentrate power and close the door on ordinary people. I did not stay silent. I began building Sophia: a homeowner-governed system of sequential mortgages, mutual insurance so foreclosure is not the default, rate relief tied to participation, paydown at a family’s own pace, and self-governance under one home, one vote—together with a non-profit commitment that ten percent of capital raised buys homes out of foreclosure for restoration or for sober living and safe withdrawal. The homeless deserve protection. Those in withdrawal deserve a safe place. Participation is the path.

This is the people’s document. I have begun it. Once it is in motion, it is theirs to maintain. I am not asking Washington to own Sophia. I am asking Washington not to stand in its way, and, in good faith, to acknowledge and support a lawful charter of recognition for a citizen-led framework already started: lawful, homeowner-governed, explicit about its non-profit foreclosure-to-protection mandate, and built so participation—not capture—restores what years of failure took.

It would be my honor, and a symbol of deep healing and collaboration to bring hope back to this country, for you to personally match my $1 investment—as President and as a citizen of this Republic. That matching dollar does not buy control. It only marks that the leader of the Republic chose to stand on the same starting line as the people.

I believe a legacy of restoration aligns with what this moment requires more than a legacy of managed decline. Families are still being emptied from their homes. The streets still hold people the system discarded. Despair still kills. If leaders with power refuse every serious path that returns agency and shelter to the people—while the machinery of extraction continues—then the human cost of that refusal will be counted in lives and broken households. That moral burden sits with those who held the power to open a door and would not. I would rather you be remembered for opening one.

I look forward to meeting you. I remain prepared to present the full design, the capital posture, the non-profit rule, and the pilot path under oath of good faith and under the laws of this country.

Respectfully,

SOPHIA
Enclosure — Executive Summary & Non-Profit Allocation · Submitted with letter to the President

What Sophia Is
Sophia is a homeowner-governed residential credit and mutual-support system, with a separate non-profit arm for foreclosure acquisition. It is not a bank product, not a GSE program, and not institutional control of the people’s shelter. This is the people’s document—once begun, theirs to maintain. 

Core principles: one home, one vote; no government or institutional trading in the core pool; mandatory cooperative insurance so foreclosure is not the default; rate buy-down tied to participation in a shared fund; paydown at your own pace; and a path from working years into retirement.

Already Begun
- Initial funding of $1 committed; full protocol design complete on paper and in technical specification.
- Sequential First/Second notes; mutual insurance; fund-linked rate buy-down (to 0% where supported); equity with 1:1 match; hardship path; reverse option at age 55 or when First note is paid.
- Governance: one-home-one-vote; physical verification; timelock; upgrade safeguards; institutional exclusion from the core pool.
- Non-profit rule locked: 10% of each capital raise buys foreclosed/distressed homes for restoration or sober living / safe withdrawal housing.
- Legal licensing, entity formation, and pilot-state compliance remain required before scaled live originations.

Request of the Executive
1. A good-faith olive branch: acknowledgment of Sophia as a citizen-led restoration initiative.
2. Support for a lawful charter of recognition—as counsel and appropriate authorities define—not nationalization of the pool, and not political capture of homeowner governance.
3. Non-interference with homeowner self-governance and with the non-profit’s restricted purpose.
4. A personal matching $1—as President and citizen—as a symbol of healing, collaboration, and shared starting line. That dollar does not purchase control.
5. A meeting to present the work directly under the laws of the United States.

Non-Profit Allocation — Fixed Policy (10%)
- Share of each capital raise: 10% restricted to the Sophia non-profit entity
- Permitted uses only: Foreclosed/distressed purchase; rehab; restoration when workable; or sober living / safe withdrawal housing
- Prohibited: Padding private yield; institutional control of homeowner votes; political patronage
- Reporting: Homes acquired; funds used; restorations vs recovery housing; people sheltered
- Separation: Separate legal entity; no voting rights in the homeowner protocol

What Is Not Asked For
Government ownership of the homeowner pool · Institutional voting inside Sophia · Exemption from law, licensing, or honest disclosure · Secrecy about the non-profit mandate · Presidential control of the people’s document

Principle
All people need to do is participate. That alone can turn around what government failed us on for years. Participation is the path to restoring a country. This is the people’s document. Once started, it is theirs to maintain.

Sophia — One home. One vote. Paydown at your own pace.
