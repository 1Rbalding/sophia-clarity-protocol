# Sophia Launch Plan

**Project:** Sophia (Clarity Protocol)  
**Date:** July 26, 2026  
**Status:** Design complete; code ready; $1 seed committed; 10% non-profit rule locked.

## Executive Summary (from Presidential Packet)
Sophia is a homeowner-governed residential credit and mutual-support system.
- One home, one vote governance.
- Sequential First/Second notes + mutual insurance (foreclosure not default).
- Rate buy-down tied to participation (can go to 0%).
- Paydown at your own pace + equity 1:1 match + hardship/reverse options.
- Separate non-profit: 10% of every capital raise buys foreclosed/distressed homes for restoration or sober living/safe withdrawal.
- Not a bank product, not GSE, not institutional control.
- Already begun with $1 + full protocol design on paper + technical specification.
