## Formal Request

Mr. President,

I respectfully request that the Executive Branch acknowledge and support a **lawful charter of recognition** for Sophia (also referred to as the Clarity Protocol).

Sophia is a homeowner-governed system of residential credit and mutual support that has already been designed and initiated with a symbolic $1 commitment. It is built on the principles of:

- One home, one vote (with physical verification and timelocks)
- Sequential notes + mandatory mutual insurance so that foreclosure is never the default
- Rate buy-down to as low as 0% through participation in a shared fund
- Flexible paydown at a family’s own pace + 1:1 equity match + hardship and reverse options
- A hard-coded, separate non-profit arm that receives 10% of every capital raise to purchase and restore foreclosed or distressed homes, or to provide sober living and safe withdrawal housing

This is the people’s document. Once begun, it is theirs to maintain. I am not asking the federal government to own Sophia, to control its governance, or to fund its operations. I am asking for a good-faith acknowledgment and a lawful charter of recognition that protects the framework’s core commitments while allowing citizen-led implementation to proceed.

## Core Commitments Locked into Sophia

1. **10% Non-Profit Rule**  
   Ten percent of every capital raise is permanently restricted to the separate Sophia non-profit entity.  
   Permitted uses only: acquisition of foreclosed/distressed properties for restoration, or creation/support of sober living and safe withdrawal housing.  
   Prohibited: any use that pads private yield, grants institutional control over homeowner votes, or serves political patronage.

2. **Homeowner Self-Governance**  
   One home equals one vote.  
   Physical verification + timelocks protect against capture.  
   Institutional actors are excluded from the core governance pool.

3. **Founder Recall Mechanism**  
   The founder begins with governance participation but remains fully subject to recall by homeowner vote at any time. The founder will proactively call for a retirement vote upon entering retirement.

4. **Protection Over Foreclosure**  
   Mandatory mutual insurance and participation-linked rate relief exist so that no family loses their home to default when participation and fund support are available.

5. **Transparency and Non-Interference**  
   All capital flows, non-profit allocations, and governance actions will be reported publicly. The charter request asks only for recognition and non-interference — not control, not nationalization, and not political capture of homeowner decisions.

1. **Lawful Charter of Recognition**  
   Formal acknowledgment by the Executive (as defined by counsel and appropriate authorities) that Sophia operates as a citizen-led, homeowner-governed restoration initiative with the commitments above.

2. **Non-Interference**  
   Assurance that homeowner self-governance and the non-profit’s restricted charitable purpose will not be obstructed.

3. **Symbolic $1 Match**  
   A personal matching dollar from the President — as citizen and leader — to stand on the same starting line as the people who have already begun this work. This dollar buys no control and no ownership.

4. **Meeting**  
   An opportunity to present the full design, the on-chain elements, the pilot path, and the legal structure under oath of good faith.

## What Is Explicitly Not Requested

- Government ownership or control of the homeowner pool  
- Institutional or political voting rights inside Sophia governance  
- Exemption from licensing, disclosure, or honest dealing requirements  
- Nationalization of the protocol or the non-profit  
- Any mechanism that would allow capture of homeowner votes or the non-profit’s mission
